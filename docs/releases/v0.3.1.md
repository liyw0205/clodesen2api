# v0.3.1

- Switch image generation and editing to the next account after an explicit upstream insufficient-credit rejection, including route allocation and initial SSE errors.
- Keep accounts with 100 credits or less in credit cooldown; recheck expired cooldowns before reuse and require a balance above 100 to recover.
- Keep scheduled check-in and redemption attempts once per day regardless of success, avoid early startup execution, and allow an explicitly saved future time to rearm today's schedule.
- Keep background account jobs running without rendering a task panel on the accounts page; manual actions still show completion feedback and refresh account data.
- Add regression coverage for account failover, metering settlement, cooldown recovery, schedule persistence, restart behavior, and the browser workflow.

This patch release follows v0.3.0. Existing v0.3.0 tags and release assets remain immutable.
